## Chapter Summaries

**Ch125 — Interview Execution Meta-Skills**
How to behave in the room: driving the conversation vs being driven, handling uncertainty, pacing across 45 minutes, and recovering when you get stuck. The most neglected chapter — most candidates know the content but not how to deliver it.

**Ch126 — Behavioral / Leadership Interview**
Google's behavioral round uses the Googleyness + Leadership rubric. This chapter covers the STAR method, 20 pre-built stories mapped to common questions, and how to calibrate story scope for L5 vs L6.

**Ch127 — Offer Negotiation**
Total comp components (base, RSU, bonus, signing), how Google's leveling affects offer ranges, negotiation tactics, and the most common mistakes candidates make. Includes the $200K negotiation gap case study from Blind (2022).

**Ch128 — Google Hiring Process Deep Dive**
The full loop from application to offer: recruiter screen, phone screen, onsite rounds, Hiring Committee review, leveling by evidence, and team matching. Includes a mock HC packet and a full interview simulation.

## The Google Hiring Timeline

```
Application
    ↓
Recruiter Screen     (30 min — background, role fit)
    ↓
Phone Screen         (45 min — 1 coding + behavioral)
    ↓
Onsite Loop          (4–5 rounds × 45 min)
  ├── Coding          (2 rounds)
  ├── System Design   (1–2 rounds)
  └── Behavioral      (1 round)
    ↓
Hiring Committee Review
    ↓
Leveling Decision
    ↓
Team Matching
    ↓
Offer
```
