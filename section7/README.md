# Section 7 — Engineering Craft

The skills that separate engineers who can design systems from engineers who can build, maintain, and evolve them. These chapters cover the "how we actually work" dimension — debugging, migrations, code review, observability, performance, and career growth. Also useful for behavioral rounds and "tell me about a time" questions.

## Core Themes

- **Debugging is a methodology** — not guessing, not panic; a repeatable diagnostic process
- **Migrations are the hardest work** — this is where Staff engineers earn their keep; most candidates skip this in interviews
- **RFCs are how Staff engineers influence without authority** — writing well is a force multiplier
- **Observability ≠ logging** — the three pillars work together; knowing the difference signals seniority
